Delete your DuoDate account

DuoDate, by [LEGAL NAME]

You can delete your DuoDate account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. To ask by email, you do not need to have the app installed.

Deleting your account also deletes your couple. Your shared dates, cover photos, fits, goals and achievements are deleted for your partner too. This cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

In the app

Go to the Us screen, tap Delete account, then hold the button to confirm. Your account is deleted right away. Delete account is also on the screen you see after a couple ends.

By email, without the app

  1. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address you use to sign in to DuoDate.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my DuoDate account".
  3. We reply to confirm that the request came from you.
  4. We delete your account within 30 days of your confirmation, and we email you when it is done.

If you can no longer use that email address, tell us when you contact us and we will ask for other details to confirm the account is yours.

What we delete

  • Your account, email address and display name.
  • Your profile photo, mood and preferences.
  • Your push tokens.
  • Your couple and everything shared in it: dates, cover photos, fits and outfit photos, goals and achievements.

What may remain for a limited time

  • Backups and logs: copies in routine backups and technical logs held by our service providers are removed on their normal schedules.
  • Legal records: we keep limited information for longer only if the law requires it, or to handle a dispute or safety report. For example, we may keep a record that your request was completed.
  • Reports: reports you sent, or that name you, stay without a link to your account.
  • Your phone: reminder settings, partner tile photos and temporary photo copies stay on your phone until you remove the app.

Only want to separate from your partner?

You do not need to delete your account. Use End couple on the Us screen. Both of you lose access to shared content right away, and it is kept for 30 days in case you both choose to reconnect. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
