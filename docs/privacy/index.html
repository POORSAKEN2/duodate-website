DuoDate is a mobile app that helps couples plan dates together. DuoDate is run by [LEGAL NAME], based in [COUNTRY] ("we" or "us"). This policy explains what information DuoDate collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

For any privacy question, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, which we use to send sign-in codes, your display name, and when you accepted our terms.
  • Profile: your profile photo, if you add one.
  • Your couple: the link between you and your partner, your invite code, and the date you got together, if you add one.
  • Dates: title, venue name and address, date and time, vibe, notes, whether the date is outdoors, its status, and a cover photo if you add one.
  • Fits: outfit photos, colour tags and descriptions you add to a date.
  • Mood: your current mood and an optional short note. A new mood replaces the previous one.
  • Preferences: the things you mark as loved, wanted or not for you.
  • Goals and achievements: shared goals, their list items and progress, and achievements you earn together.
  • Reports: the reason and details you send if you report someone.

DuoDate does not ask for sensitive information such as health details. Please do not enter it in notes or other text fields.

Information collected automatically

  • Push token: if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send notifications to your phone.
  • Technical logs: when the app connects to our servers, our hosting provider records technical information such as IP address, request details and sign-in times.

Information kept only on your phone

Your reminder settings, your cover scene, and the photos you add to your partner tile stay on your phone. DuoDate also keeps temporary copies of shared photos on your phone so they load faster. Removing the app removes this information.

Permissions

DuoDate uses your camera and photo library only when you choose to add a photo. Only the photo you choose is compressed and uploaded. Notifications are optional.

What DuoDate does not collect

DuoDate does not collect your device location, your contacts or payment details. DuoDate contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

How we use information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show and sync your content between you and your partner.
  • To send notifications, such as when your partner adds a date.
  • To keep DuoDate secure, prevent abuse and act on reports.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who receives your information

Your partner

Once you pair, your partner can see your display name, profile photo, mood and preferences, and the dates, fits, goals and achievements in your couple.

Service providers

These companies process information for us so that DuoDate can work:

  • Supabase provides our database, sign-in, photo storage and server functions. Your information is stored in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails and receives your email address and sign-in code. It also delivers report alerts to us, which contain the report and the account ids involved.
  • Expo sends push notifications and receives your push token and the notification text, which can include a display name and a venue name.
  • Apple and Google deliver push notifications to your phone.

Legal reasons and business transfers

We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when it is necessary to protect someone's safety. If DuoDate is sold or transferred to a new owner, your information may transfer with it, and we will tell you first.

International transfers

Your information is stored in Japan and our service providers may process it in other countries, including the United States. These countries may have different data protection laws from yours. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

How long we keep information

  • Your account: we keep your account information until you delete your account.
  • When a couple ends: both partners lose access to shared data right away. We keep the shared data for 30 days so that you can both choose to reconnect. After 30 days, or as soon as either person pairs with someone new, a daily clean-up permanently deletes the shared dates, fits, goals, achievements and photo files. Each person's profile photo, mood and preferences stay with their own account.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your information and your couple. This also deletes the couple's shared information for your partner. See how to delete your account.
  • Push tokens: we keep a token until it stops working or your account is deleted.
  • Reports: we keep reports to handle safety concerns. If an account is deleted, the report stays without a link to that account.
  • Backups and logs: copies in routine backups and technical logs held by our service providers are removed on their normal schedules.
  • Legal retention: we keep limited information for longer only if the law requires it, or to handle a dispute or safety report. For example, we may keep a record that a deletion request was completed.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Access rules limit your information to you and your partner. Photos are kept in private storage and are opened through time-limited links. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your mood and preferences in the app.
  • Turn off notifications, camera or photo access in your phone settings.
  • End your couple from the Us screen.
  • Delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We respond within 30 days.

Legal bases in the EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use your information because it is necessary to provide DuoDate under our contract with you, for our legitimate interests in keeping DuoDate secure and preventing abuse, to comply with the law, and with your consent for optional permissions such as notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in your phone settings.

Children

DuoDate is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. If we learn that we hold information about someone under 18, we delete it.
